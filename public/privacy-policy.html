Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Marathi ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal information you share while using our mobile application. This Privacy Policy outlines what data we collect, how it is used, and your rights.

2. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information strictly necessary to deliver and personalize your learning experience:

  • Account Information: Mobile phone number for OTP verification, optional name, and learning preferences.
  • Study & Progress Data: Question scores, completed flashcards, bookmark favorites, and daily practice streaks.
  • Device & Diagnostics: Anonymous diagnostic logs and OS versions to fix bugs and improve performance.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to:

  • Save and synchronize your study progress across app launches.
  • Provide real-time score calculation and tailored question recommendations.
  • Ensure account security via one-time SMS passwords (OTP).
  • Provide prompt customer support when requested.

4. Data Sharing & Security

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data with third-party advertisers. All network communications are encrypted with industry-standard TLS protocols. We retain your practice data only for as long as your account remains active.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions or wish to request data deletion, please contact our privacy team at: support@marathiapp.in.
